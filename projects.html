Women Empowerment

Catalyzing economic sovereignty and banking inclusion for 450+ rural Dalit and Bahujan women across Sirivella and Rayalaseema mandals.

ACTIVE INITIATIVE • SIRIVELLA BLOCK

RURAL WOMEN'S BANKING & MICRO-ENTERPRISE DEVELOPMENT

Supported by a ₹33.19 Lakh grant from the Grace TPTC Foundation in FY 2024–25, this 12-month program delivers structured financial literacy, digital UPI banking setup, and micro-business credit management to women across Sirivella Block. By transitioning female household heads from vulnerable informal borrowing to bank accounts and small commercial enterprises (grocery stores, livestock rearing, and tailoring shops), we foster multi-generational economic resilience and household stability.

Child Education

Preventing primary school dropouts and securing classroom retention for first-generation rural Dalit learners across Kurnool mandals.

10 RURAL GOVERNMENT SCHOOLS

PRIMARY SCHOOL RETENTION & EDUCATIONAL EQUALITY CAMPAIGNS

Operating across 10 government primary schools in Bandi Atmakur and Sirvel mandals, DWA provides complete textbook kits, uniforms, school bags, and evening tutorial sessions. We work closely with village school management committees (SMCs) and local teachers to identify at-risk children early, preventing seasonal harvest dropouts and ensuring every girl and boy receives continuous foundational schooling and daily nutritional support.

Orphanage & Child Care

Safe residential sanctuary, nutritious meals, healthcare, and trauma-informed support for rural parentless children.

COMPASSIONATE FOSTER PROTECTION

LOVING RESIDENTIAL FOSTER CARE & NUTRITIONAL SECURITY

For children who have lost parents to disease, agricultural debt, or family tragedies, DWA provides a safe, stigma-free home rooted in dignity. Our dedicated staff and caregivers ensure every child receives balanced daily nutrition, medical monitoring, formal school enrollment, and moral mentorship. We guarantee that no child under our care is forced into child labor or street vulnerability, cultivating an environment where their individual talents can thrive.

DWA Privacy & Data Policy

1. Our Commitment to Donor Privacy: Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect personal donor information (such as name, address, email, phone number, and PAN or passport details) strictly for statutory FCRA and Section 80G compliance as required by the Ministry of Home Affairs and Income Tax Department, Government of India.

2. No Commercial Sharing or Sale: We never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party marketers or commercial organizations. Information is used exclusively to generate official 80G tax exemption receipts (Form 10BE), send quarterly field bulletins (if opted in), and satisfy statutory CA audit mandates.

3. Data Security: Donor transaction data transmitted over digital gateways (such as Benevity or banking conduits) is processed via secure 256-bit SSL encryption. Statutory accounting records are securely stored and retained in compliance with Indian non-profit governance regulations.

4. Contact & Inquiries: If you wish to update your donor record, request deletion of subscription details, or have questions regarding your data, please contact our administrative office at dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com.

DWA Donation Refund Policy

1. Nature of Charitable Contributions: Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) is a registered charitable non-profit society (Reg No. 384/1993). As all donations are voluntary contributions towards social welfare, livelihood empowerment, and educational programs, donations are generally considered non-refundable once deployed into community field initiatives.

2. Erroneous or Duplicate Deductions: If an unintended duplicate transaction occurs or an error was made in the donation amount due to a technical glitch, please notify us within 7 calendar days of the transaction date with full transaction proof, transaction reference number, and payment receipt.

3. Review & Processing: Genuine grievance claims for erroneous or unauthorized transactions will be verified against our banking records with State Bank of India. Once verified, refunds will be processed via original payment channel within 10 to 14 business days. Please note that if an official 80G tax receipt has already been filed with the Income Tax Department, statutory adjustments may apply.

4. Inquiries & Requests: To request a donation verification or refund inquiry, write to dalitwelfareassociation1@gmail.com or call our registered office at +91 8309744864.
