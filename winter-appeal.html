01 • Field Reality in Rural Kurnool

Drought, Food Inflation & Distress Migration

Your holiday gift intervenes directly against three compounding emergencies gripping landless rural households across 20+ habitations.

Parched Earth & Agrarian Collapse

In Rayalaseema’s semi-arid Kurnool belt, prolonged El Niño weather anomalies have drained wells, withered subsistence crops, and eliminated local farm day-labor—while post-drought inflation puts staple food out of reach.

300 km Flight to Chilli Labor Camps

Seeking survival wages, families migrate over 300 kilometers to commercial chilli farms in Guntur—living in makeshift plastic tarpaulin tents without clean water, sanitation, or protection from pesticide dust.

Children Pulled Out of School

Young pupils are withdrawn from village government primary schools to accompany migrating parents, exposing them to permanent dropout and hazardous manual child labor.

Frail Elders Left Behind

Bedridden and elderly grandparents unable to travel remain behind in nearly deserted villages with scarce food, inadequate medicine, and zero protection against cold winter night temperatures.
