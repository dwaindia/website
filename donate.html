05 • Partner With Us

CSR Partnerships, Institutional Grants & Volunteering

Corporate CSR Partnerships

We invite Indian companies to explore partnerships in rural livelihoods, women’s empowerment, education, health, and environmental awareness. Proposals specify objectives, beneficiaries, timelines, M&E indicators, and Schedule VII alignment under the Companies Act, 2013.

Institutional Grants & Foundations

DWA collaborates with domestic and international foundations on clearly scoped initiatives supported by due diligence, statutory documentation, baseline/endline records, and independent CA reporting under FCRA compliance where applicable.

Volunteer & Pro-Bono Skills

Contribute expertise in vocational & business skills training, financial literacy, digital payment coaching, educational mentoring, monitoring & evaluation, or communications & organizational capacity building.
