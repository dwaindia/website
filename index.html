Working in Andhra Pradesh Since 1993

Building Dignity. Creating Opportunity. Strengthening Communities.

Since 1993, Dalit Welfare Association (DWA) has worked alongside marginalized and rural families in Kurnool and Nandyal districts of Andhra Pradesh—advancing sustainable women’s livelihoods, child education, water resilience, and community-led development.

Our Institutional Purpose

Vision and Mission

Motto: “Stronger Together”

Our Vision

A society in which rural communities can live with dignity and opportunity, and where people are empowered socially and economically regardless of caste, creed, gender, or background.

Our Mission

To work alongside rural and marginalized families by creating sustainable livelihood opportunities, supporting primary education, strengthening community institutions, and promoting fair and transparent development initiatives.

Operating across Parnapalli Village, Bandi Atmakur Mandal, Sirivella Block, Gospadu Block, and Rudravaram in Andhra Pradesh. Read our story, leadership & institutional profile

Priority Focus Areas

Featured Programmes

DWA combines practical vocational assets, educational support, water stewardship, and grassroots mobilization to help rural households build lasting self-reliance.

Priority 01 • Sirivella & Rudravaram

Women’s Empowerment and Livelihoods

Supporting vulnerable rural women—including widows, single mothers, and BPL households—with practical skills in tailoring, drought-hardy sheep rearing, micro-retail shops, and dairy development alongside formal banking, UPI digital literacy, and Self-Help Group savings.

Explore Women’s Livelihoods
Priority 02 • 10 Primary Schools & Hostel Care

Child Education and Advancement

Reducing seasonal migration dropouts across 10 rural Government Primary Schools through school bags, notebooks, uniforms, merit scholarships, and inter-school competitions, alongside safe hostel support for vulnerable Budagajangala children in collaboration with Star Society in Nandyal.

Explore Child Education
Priority 03 • Semi-Arid Rayalaseema Basin

Water Conservation and Climate Adaptation

Strengthening rural resilience against recurring dry spells and groundwater depletion through community irrigation wells, monsoon rainwater recharge pits, village solid waste composting drives, and potable water hygiene education.

Explore Water & Climate Work
Priority 04 • Rural Welfare & Resilience

Community Development and Welfare

Equipping marginal farmers with dryland farming orientation and fodder banks, hosting preventive health and traditional medicinal plant workshops, mobilizing Self-Help Groups, and distributing emergency food rations and essential care during crises.

Explore Community Welfare
